AI Usage Documentation
1. Which model you used and why

In this project, I used OpenAI. It was selected because it is a reliable industry standard with proven capabilities in generating structured data and parsing logic. I plan to evaluate and potentially migrate to other models in the future to find the most cost-efficient and suitable AI for these specific testing needs.

2. Where AI runs

During Development: AI acted as a thought partner to assist with framework architecture and script debugging.

During Test Execution: AI is utilized dynamically to generate coherent dummy data for the "add customer" flow.

Post-Execution (Triage): AI analyzes the logs of failed tests to provide a root-cause summary and actionable insights regarding the failure.

3. The exact prompts you send

Data Generation for add customer flow: "Generate coherent, realistic Indonesian business data in strict JSON format. Fields needed: company_name, company_email, company_phone, company_address, industry, customer_name, customer_contact, customer_address."

Triage Analysis: "You are an expert QA Automation Engineer diagnosing test failures. Analyze the provided error trace strictly in this order: 1. Is it an exception (element not found) or a failed assertion? Exception = Script/Environment issue. 2. Did the locator resolve? 3. Did preconditions succeed? 4. Was the expected value correct? 5. Is it flaky? Verdict options: [Script/Environment Defect], [Product Bug], [Flaky]. Provide the verdict and 2-3 sentences of evidence. DO NOT auto-close or rewrite logic."

4. What happens when the AI is unavailable or returns something invalid

The framework uses Pydantic to validate the JSON schema generated by the AI. If the output is malformed, it retries up to 2 times. If it fails, hits rate limits, or if the OPENAI_API_KEY is not present, it triggers a deterministic Faker id_ID fallback. This guarantees the CI/CD pipeline runs flawlessly even in fully offline environments.

5. What you deliberately did not let AI do, and why

The AI is strictly isolated from the assertion mechanics. It cannot rewrite assertions, mask failures, or execute auto-healing on locators at runtime. This isolation ensures the integrity of the test results and prevents the AI from generating false positives.
